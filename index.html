Favorite hobbies

Watching UFC

Charles Oliveira

Charles “Do Bronxs” Oliveira is my favorite UFC fighter. He grew up in a family of modest means in Guarujá, Brazil, and faced health challenges as a kid. At 12, a community program introduced him to Brazilian jiu-jitsu, which set him on the path to becoming UFC lightweight champion.

His story goes beyond the title. He started his own program in Guarujá to give local kids the same chance to train that he had.
